Press Sign in. With a remembered sign-in 🔑 it switches in one click.

How do I switch accounts in one click (like TcNo)?

Each account is saved once. If you use TcNo, press Import from TcNo below and pick which TcNo account is which. Otherwise sign in to the account in Riot Client with "Stay signed in" ticked, then press Sign in on it in Tune and choose Save the current sign-in. From then on, quit VALORANT, pick any 🔑 account and press Sign in: Tune closes Riot Client (never the game), switches, and starts VALORANT.

Is a remembered sign-in safe?

Tune never sees your password. It keeps a copy of Riot Client's own "Stay signed in" file, the same thing TcNo does. It's encrypted so only your Windows user on this PC can use it. Anyone using your Windows account could still sign in with it, so lock your PC. "Forget saved sign-ins" below deletes them all.

How do I use a crosshair code?

Open the Crosshairs tab, press Copy on one (or paste a code from a website or a friend and save it). In VALORANT go to Settings → Crosshair, press Import profile code and paste.

My settings changed back after I signed in. Why?

Riot keeps an online copy of sensitivity and crosshair and may restore it when you sign in. Tune checks again and marks the account as changed. Press Apply again with VALORANT closed or on another account.

How do I undo?

Open the account and press "Undo last apply". Every apply makes a backup first. You can also open the backups folder below.
