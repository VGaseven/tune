Help & about
Tune copies your main account's settings onto your other accounts.
1Pick an account

Choose it on the left. You'll see its own settings, with your main's value next to anything that's different.

2Apply settings

Copies your main's sensitivity, crosshair and graphics. It's backed up first, so you can always undo.

3Sign in & play

Riot Client opens. Sign out there and sign in to that account, then play.

Why doesn't Sign in log me into the account automatically?

Signing in for you would mean handling your password or copying Riot's login files. Tune never does either, to keep your accounts safe. Switch accounts in Riot Client: profile menu, Sign out, then sign in.

My settings changed back after I signed in. Why?

Riot keeps an online copy of sensitivity and crosshair and may restore it when you sign in. Tune checks again and marks the account as changed. Press Apply again with VALORANT closed or on another account.

Do I need to close VALORANT?

No. Only the account VALORANT is currently signed in to can't be changed, because the game would overwrite it when it closes. Its card shows IN GAME.

Is it safe for my account?

Tune only edits VALORANT's own settings files on this PC, the same ones the game's settings menu writes. It doesn't touch the game while it runs, read your password, or use any login data. It can't promise anything on Riot's behalf.

How do I undo?

Select the account and press "Undo last apply". Every apply makes a backup first. You can also open the backups folder below.
